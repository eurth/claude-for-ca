| 11th of month | GSTR-1 (monthly filers, turnover > Rs.5 Cr or opted) | Monthly GSTR-1 filers | Rs.50/day (nil-rated: Rs.20/day) |
| 13th of month | GSTR-1 (QRMP — IFF optional for Q months) | QRMP filers | — |
| 13th of month | GSTR-6 (Input Service Distributor) | ISD registrants | Rs.50/day |

| 15th of month | ESIC contribution deposit | Establishments ≥ 10 employees | 12% p.a. simple interest |

## Key Penalty Reference

| Default | Penalty / Interest |
|---|---|
| Late ITR filing (u/s 234F) | Rs.1,000 (income ≤ Rs.5L) / Rs.5,000 (income > Rs.5L) |
| Non-filing of tax audit (u/s 271B) | 0.5% of turnover / gross receipts (max Rs.1,50,000) |
| Late TDS deposit (u/s 201(1A)) | 1.5% per month from date of deduction |
| Late TDS return (u/s 234E) | Rs.200 per day (max equal to TDS amount) |
| Late GSTR-1 filing | Rs.50/day (Rs.20/day for nil return) |
| Late GSTR-3B filing | Rs.50/day (Rs.20/day for nil) |
| Late GST payment | 18% per annum simple interest |
| Late AOC-4 / MGT-7 (MCA) | Rs.100 per day per form (no maximum) |
| Late DPT-3 | Rs.1 lakh + Rs.500/day continuation |
| MSME-1 non-filing | Rs.25,000 (company) + Rs.25,000 (officer) |
