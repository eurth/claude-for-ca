# Charge Registry — CHG Forms

**FINANCIAL INTEGRITY**: Charges must be filed within 30 days of creation. Late filing requires condonation at Registrar (up to 300 days) or CLB/NCLT. Unregistered charges are void against liquidator and creditors (Section 77).

## Charge Filing Quick Reference

| Event | Form | Timeline | Late Fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Creation of charge | CHG-1 | Within 30 days | Additional fee for late filing |
| Modification of charge | CHG-1 (modified) | Within 30 days | Additional fee |
| Satisfaction of charge | CHG-4 | Within 30 days | Additional fee |
| Condonation (up to 300 days) | CHG-8 | Up to 300 days | By RoC |

## Late Filing Fees (Charge Forms)

Beyond 30 days from creation/modification:
- Days 31-60: 2× normal fee
- Days 61-90: 4× normal fee
- Days 91-180: 6× normal fee
- Days 181-300: 10× normal fee
- Beyond 300 days: Application to NCLT (Section 87)

## CHG-4 — Satisfaction of Charge

When bank loan is repaid and charge needs to be released:
- Obtain NOC from charge holder (bank's letter confirming full repayment)
- File CHG-4 within 30 days of repayment date
- Charge continues on Register until CHG-4 is filed + accepted

## Due Diligence — Charge Search

For buyer/investor due diligence:
- Download charge certificate from MCA21: Company Search → Charges
- Review all open charges: amount, charge holder, date, assets covered
- Verify satisfaction status for any old charges
- Flag any undisclosed charges
