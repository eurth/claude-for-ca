# Form 16 / 16A Preparation

**FINANCIAL INTEGRITY**: Form 16 is issued under Section 203 of the Income Tax Act. Incorrect Form 16 leads to employee filing errors, notices, and potential penalty on the employer. Verify all figures against salary register and TRACES before issuing.

## Form 16 — Salary TDS

### Part A (from TRACES)
Download from TRACES → Form 16 → Certificate No. for each employee.
Part A contains: TDS deducted quarter-wise, challan details — this is auto-generated by TRACES.

### Batch Processing for All Employees

Accept salary register (Excel / CSV) with columns:
Employee | PAN | Basic | HRA | LTA | Special | Bonus | PF | Professional Tax | Deductions (80C etc.)

Generate Form 16 Part B for each employee in batch.

## Form 16A — Non-Salary TDS Certificate

For each deductee who received non-salary payments (contractors, professionals, rent):
- TRACES generates Form 16A automatically after TDS return is filed
- Download from TRACES and review for accuracy
- Issue to deductee within 15 days of end of the quarter (i.e. 15 Aug, 15 Nov, 15 Feb, 15 Jun)

```
FORM 16A CHECK:
Party: ABC Services | PAN: XXXXX | Section: 194J
Quarter | Payment Date | Amount | TDS | Challan | Status
Q1      | 15 Apr       | 50,000 | 5,000 | 07 May 2026 | ✓ Deposited
Q2      | 20 Jul       | 40,000 | 4,000 | 07 Aug 2026 | ✓ Deposited
Total TDS: Rs.9,000 | Form 16A to issue by: 15 Aug (Q2) / 15 Nov (Q3) etc.
```

## Common Errors to Check

- [ ] PAN in Form 16 matches PAN in TRACES — Mismatch causes 20% TDS recovery
- [ ] HRA exemption calculation correct — check if employee actually pays rent
- [ ] Professional tax deducted correct amount — varies by state
- [ ] Salary paid in April must be included in the year's Form 16 (advance salary / arrears)
- [ ] Any salary revision — updated in all months
- [ ] Perquisites valued correctly (car, ESOPs, low-interest loans)
