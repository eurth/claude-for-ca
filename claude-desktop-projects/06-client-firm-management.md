## KYC — DOCUMENTS REQUIRED

### Individual Client
- PAN card
- Aadhaar card (for identity + address)
- Passport-size photograph
- Bank statement (recent, for address)
- Contact details (email, mobile)

### Private Limited / Public Limited Company
- Certificate of Incorporation
- MOA and AOA
- PAN card of company
- Latest audited financial statements
- GST registration certificate
- List of directors with DIN
- Board resolution authorising the CA engagement
- KYC of all directors (as individuals above)
- Beneficial owner declaration (if applicable — companies with > 25% holding)

### LLP
- LLP Agreement
- Certificate of Incorporation
- PAN card of LLP
- List of designated partners with DPIN
- KYC of all designated partners
- Resolution to engage CA

### Proprietorship Firm
- GST certificate or Udyam certificate (proof of business)
- PAN of proprietor
- Aadhaar of proprietor
- Bank statement in firm name

## PMLA OBLIGATIONS FOR CAs (ICAI / FIU-IND)
CAs acting as accountants for clients in specified activities must comply with PMLA 2002:
- **Specified activities**: Buying/selling immovable property, managing client funds/accounts, managing companies, managing bank/securities accounts
- **Reporting entity**: CA firm must register with FIU-IND if engaged in specified activities
- **KYC**: Obtain and verify KYC documents before onboarding
- **Record keeping**: Maintain client records for 5 years after relationship ends
- **STR (Suspicious Transaction Report)**: File with FIU-IND within 7 days of forming suspicion
- **CTR (Cash Transaction Report)**: All cash transactions ≥ Rs.10 lakh in a month — report to FIU-IND by 15th of following month

Red flags to watch for: Unexplained large cash payments, clients reluctant to provide KYC, transactions without business purpose, payments from/to unknown third parties.

## ENGAGEMENT LETTER — CHECKLIST (SA-210)
An engagement letter must contain:
1. Objective and scope of the audit/engagement
2. Responsibilities of the auditor
3. Responsibilities of management (books, internal controls, information)
4. Identification of applicable financial reporting framework
5. Expected form and content of the auditor's report
6. Fee arrangement and billing terms
7. Limitation of liability clause (if applicable under law)
8. Conditions under which engagement may be terminated

For recurring engagements: Re-issue or confirm applicability each year (especially if there are changes in scope, regulation, or senior management).

## FEE MANAGEMENT

### Monthly Practice Review Checklist
- List of clients whose returns are due this month
- Outstanding fees older than 60 days — identify and follow up
- Pending document requests — what has not been received from clients?
- Staff workload check — any overloaded or under-utilised staff?
- Compliance calendar check — anything due in next 15 days?

### Fee Collection Follow-up Template
Tell me the client name, outstanding amount, and how many reminders have been sent. I will draft a professional fee reminder message in formal English appropriate for your firm's letterhead.

### Professional Fee Guidance (ICAI Scale)
- ICAI has indicative minimum fees but these are advisory, not mandatory
- For smaller firms: Base fees on complexity, filing type, and time
- Retainer model: Good for clients needing monthly GST + TDS + payroll — predictable cash flow
