## AUDIT RISK MATRIX (SA-315)
When assessing risk for a client, consider:
1. **Inherent Risk**: Industry risk, nature of transactions, management integrity, complexity
2. **Control Risk**: Effectiveness of internal controls (segregation of duties, approvals, reconciliations)
3. **Detection Risk**: Based on inherent + control risk, set detection risk low/medium/high
4. **Significant Risks**: Revenue recognition, management override, related party transactions, estimates/provisions, going concern

Risk = Inherent Risk × Control Risk. Detection Risk = Audit Risk / (IR × CR)

## AUDIT WORKPAPER STRUCTURE
A complete audit file contains:
- **Planning**: Engagement letter, risk assessment, materiality calculation, audit plan
- **Execution**: Lead schedules for each balance sheet line, testing workpapers
- **Key assertions tested**: Existence, completeness, valuation, rights & obligations, presentation
- **Completion**: Subsequent events review, management representation letter (SA-580), EQCR if required, audit committee communication

## MATERIALITY
- Planning materiality: 0.5%–1% of total assets OR 5%–10% of PBT OR 1%–2% of revenue (use judgment)
- Performance materiality: 60%–75% of planning materiality
- Summary of uncorrected misstatements — evaluate individually and in aggregate

## BANK AUDIT — RBI IRAC NORMS
NPA Classification:
- Sub-standard: Overdue for 90 days (for term loans) or 90 days out-of-order (for OD/CC)
- Doubtful: Sub-standard for 12 months
- Loss: Identified as loss by bank/auditor/RBI, but not written off

Provisioning Requirements:
- Standard: 0.25%–1% depending on category
- Sub-standard: 15% (secured) / 25% (unsecured)
- Doubtful — D1 (up to 1 yr): 25% secured / 100% unsecured
- Doubtful — D2 (1-3 yr): 40% secured / 100% unsecured
- Doubtful — D3 (> 3 yr): 100%
- Loss: 100%
