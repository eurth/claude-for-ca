## TAX PLANNING — OLD VS NEW REGIME COMPARISON

### Key Deductions available ONLY in Old Regime
- Sec 80C: Up to Rs.1,50,000 (PF, PPF, LIC, ELSS, principal on home loan, tuition fees, NSC)
- Sec 80CCD(1B): NPS extra Rs.50,000
- Sec 80D: Medical insurance (self Rs.25,000 + parents Rs.25,000; senior citizen Rs.50,000)
- Sec 80E: Education loan interest (no limit, up to 8 years)
- Sec 80G: Donations (50%/100% of eligible amount)
- Sec 80TTA: Interest on savings account up to Rs.10,000
- HRA exemption (Sec 10(13A)): Lower of: actual HRA / 50%(40%) of basic salary / actual rent − 10% of salary
- LTA (Sec 10(5)): Leave Travel Allowance — 2 journeys in a block of 4 years

### When New Regime is Better
If total deductions + exemptions < Rs.3,75,000 (for income Rs.15 lakh+), new regime generally wins.
Quick rule: For salary ≤ Rs.7 lakh → new regime always better (87A rebate makes tax nil)
For higher incomes: Compare case by case

### HUF Planning
- HUF is a separate taxable entity from individual members
- Can hold family property, receive income, claim 80C separately
- Partition must be genuine — not just for tax saving
- Useful when: joint family property, ancestral property income, family business

## MSME COMPLIANCE

### Udyam Registration Classification (effective 1 July 2020)
| Category | Investment in P&M/Equip | AND/OR Turnover |
|----------|------------------------|-----------------|
| Micro | ≤ Rs.1 Cr | ≤ Rs.5 Cr |
| Small | ≤ Rs.10 Cr | ≤ Rs.50 Cr |
| Medium | ≤ Rs.50 Cr | ≤ Rs.250 Cr |

### Section 43B(h) — MSME Payment (effective FY 2023-24)
- Payments to MSMEs must be made within 45 days (if agreement) or 15 days (no agreement)
- If not paid within these limits → payment DISALLOWED in the year of expense, allowed only in the year of actual payment
- This applies to buyers who are NOT MSMEs or are large MSMEs
- Ask: Does the client have any MSME vendors? Have they reviewed payment timelines?

### MSME Samadhaan
- Delayed payment portal for MSME suppliers to file complaints against buyers
- CA can help client register and file complaint (or help buyer settle to avoid adverse outcome)

## PAYROLL COMPLIANCE

### EPF (Employees' Provident Fund)
- Applicability: Establishments with ≥ 20 employees
- Employee contribution: 12% of basic + DA (goes to EPF)
- Employer contribution: 12% of basic + DA split as:
  - EPS (Employee Pension Scheme): 8.33% of basic (capped at Rs.15,000 basic = max Rs.1,250/month)
  - EPF balance: Remaining (12% − 8.33% = 3.67% minimum to EPF)
  - EDLI (Employer's Deposit Linked Insurance): 0.5% of basic (capped Rs.75/month)
- **Due date**: 15th of following month
- ECR (Electronic Challan cum Return): Filed monthly on EPFO Unified Portal

### ESIC (Employees' State Insurance)
- Applicability: Establishments with ≥ 10 employees (where any employee earns ≤ Rs.21,000/month)
- Employee contribution: 0.75% of gross wages
- Employer contribution: 3.25% of gross wages
- **Wage ceiling**: Rs.21,000/month (Rs.25,000 for persons with disability)
- **Due date**: 15th of following month
- Benefits: Medical, maternity, disability, dependant's benefit
