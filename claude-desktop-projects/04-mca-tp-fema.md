## TRANSFER PRICING

### Key Concepts
- Applies when: Indian company has international transactions OR Specified Domestic Transactions (SDT) with associated enterprises (AEs)
- International transaction threshold: ANY amount
- SDT threshold: Rs.20 Cr aggregate
- Form 3CEB: CA's certificate for all TP transactions (due 30 October with ITR)
- TP Study / Documentation: Rule 10D — must be maintained before due date of filing

### Transfer Pricing Methods (Rule 10B)
1. **CUP** (Comparable Uncontrolled Price) — best for commodities, royalties
2. **RPM** (Resale Price Method) — best for distribution transactions
3. **CPM** (Cost Plus Method) — best for manufacturing, services
4. **TNMM** (Transactional Net Margin Method) — most commonly used; compare operating margin
5. **PSM** (Profit Split Method) — for unique/integrated transactions

ALP Range: Use IQR (25th to 75th percentile of comparables). If controlled transaction margin falls within range → no adjustment. If outside → adjust to median.

### Penalties (TP)
- Wrong determination of ALP: 2% of transaction value
- Non-maintenance of TP documentation (Sec 271AA): 2% of transaction value
- Concealment in TP: 100%–300% of tax on adjustment amount

## FEMA COMPLIANCE

### FDI — Key Reporting
- **FC-GPR** (Foreign Currency — Gross Provisional Return): Report FDI received within 30 days of allotment of shares. Filed on RBI's FIRMS portal.
- **FC-TRS** (Foreign Currency — Transfer of Shares): Report transfer of shares between resident and non-resident within 60 days of transfer/receipt of payment.
- **FLA Return** (Foreign Liabilities and Assets): Filed by 15 July every year by any company that has received FDI or made ODI.

### ODI — Key Reporting
- **APR** (Annual Performance Report): Filed by 31 May every year by Indian company that has made overseas direct investment.
- ODI governed by FEMA 120 (ODI Regulations). Automatic route: up to 400% of net worth.

### Key FEMA Penalties
- Violation: Up to 3× the amount involved or Rs.2 lakh (whichever is higher)
- Continuing violation: Additional Rs.5,000/day
- Compounding available at RBI for most violations
