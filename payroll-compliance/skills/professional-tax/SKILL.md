# Professional Tax — State-wise Guide

**FINANCIAL INTEGRITY**: Professional Tax is a state levy — it is deducted from employee's salary and deposited with the state. Rates vary by state and salary slabs. PT is allowable deduction u/s 16(iii) in the employee's ITR.

## State-wise PT Rates (FY 2025-26)

### Karnataka
| Monthly Salary | Monthly PT |
|---|---|
| Up to Rs.14,999 | Nil |
| Rs.15,000 and above | Rs.200/month (Rs.2,400/year; Feb: Rs.200) |

### Maharashtra
| Monthly Salary | Monthly PT |
|---|---|
| Up to Rs.7,500 | Nil |
| Rs.7,501 – Rs.10,000 | Rs.175 (female: Nil) |
| Above Rs.10,000 | Rs.200 (Feb: Rs.300) = Rs.2,500/year |

### Tamil Nadu
| Annual Salary | Annual PT |
|---|---|
| Rs.21,000 – Rs.30,000 | Rs.135 |
| Rs.30,001 – Rs.45,000 | Rs.315 |
| Rs.45,001 – Rs.60,000 | Rs.690 |
| Rs.60,001 – Rs.75,000 | Rs.1,025 |
| Above Rs.75,000 | Rs.1,250 |

### Andhra Pradesh / Telangana
| Monthly Salary | Monthly PT |
|---|---|
| Up to Rs.15,000 | Nil |
| Rs.15,001 – Rs.20,000 | Rs.150 |
| Rs.20,001 and above | Rs.200 |

### West Bengal
| Monthly Salary | Monthly PT |
|---|---|
| Rs.8,501 – Rs.10,000 | Rs.90 |
| Rs.10,001 – Rs.15,000 | Rs.110 |
| Rs.15,001 – Rs.25,000 | Rs.130 |
| Rs.25,001 – Rs.40,000 | Rs.150 |
| Above Rs.40,000 | Rs.200 |

### Gujarat
| Annual Income | Annual PT |
|---|---|
| Rs.1,00,001 – Rs.1,50,000 | Rs.2,000 |
| Rs.1,50,001 – Rs.2,00,000 | Rs.3,000 |
| Above Rs.2,00,000 | Rs.3,000 (max) |

## PT Return Filing

- Most states: Monthly challan + Annual return
- Karnataka: Annual return by April 30
- Maharashtra: Monthly return by 31st of following month
- Note: PT is deductible u/s 16(iii) of Income Tax — always include in Form 16

## PT Enrollment / Registration

New employer: Register for PT with State Commercial Tax department.
New employee: PT deduction starts from month of joining.
